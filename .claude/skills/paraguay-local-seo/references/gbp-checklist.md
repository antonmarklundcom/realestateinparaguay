# Off-Site Local SEO Checklist — Paraguay

Hand this (adapted to the specific business) to the user after delivering the website. The website is ~30% of local ranking; Google Business Profile (GBP) and reviews are the rest.

## 1. Google Business Profile (Perfil de Negocio de Google)

- Create/claim at business.google.com. Verification usually via video or postcard.
- **Business name**: exact real name only. Do NOT stuff keywords ("Carpintería López" ✓, "Carpintería López – Muebles a Medida Asunción" ✗ — risks suspension).
- **Primary category** in Spanish: pick the most specific ("Dentista", "Carpintero", "Fontanero" — Google uses "Fontanero" for plomero, "Electricista", "Taller de reparación de automóviles", "Peluquería", "Abogado"). Add 2–4 secondary categories.
- **Service-area business** (carpinteros, plomeros who travel): hide the street address, set service areas to the actual cities served (max ~20).
- **Storefront business** (dental clinic, salon): show address; it must match the website footer NAP exactly, character for character.
- Phone: the same +595 number as the website. Website field: the new site URL.
- Hours: accurate, including Saturday (many PY trades work Saturday mornings).
- **Photos**: minimum 10 real photos — exterior, interior, team, work in progress, finished jobs. Profiles with real photos get dramatically more calls. Add 2+ new photos monthly.
- **Services section**: list every service with a short description and price range in ₲ where possible ("Desde ₲ 150.000").
- Enable **messaging/chat** and respond fast — response speed affects conversion.
- Post updates (Novedades) 2–4×/month: finished jobs, promos, tips.

## 2. Reviews (reseñas) — the #1 local ranking lever

- Goal: steady flow, not a burst. 2–5 new reviews/month beats 30 in one week.
- After every completed job, send the customer the direct review link via WhatsApp (get it from GBP → "Solicitar reseñas"). Suggested message the business can copy:
  > "¡Gracias por confiar en nosotros! Si quedaste conforme con el trabajo, nos ayudaría muchísimo una reseña en Google: [link] 🙏"
- Ask happy customers to mention the service and city in the review ("Excelente carpintero, me hizo un placar a medida en Lambaré") — keyword-rich reviews boost relevance.
- Respond to EVERY review in Spanish within 48h, positive or negative. Never argue publicly with negative ones; apologize, offer to resolve via WhatsApp.
- Never buy or fabricate reviews — suspension risk and it's dishonest.

## 3. Citations & directories (consistent NAP everywhere)

Same name, address, phone format everywhere (+595 9XX XXX XXX). Relevant for Paraguay:

- Facebook Business Page (essential — many Paraguayans search services on Facebook)
- Instagram business profile with city in bio + link to site
- Waze (for storefronts)
- Bing Places (low effort, why not)
- Páginas Amarillas Paraguay / local online directories that are active
- Trade associations where applicable (Círculo de Odontólogos del Paraguay for dentists, Colegio de Abogados, etc.) — member listings are strong trust citations
- Marketplace presence can help visibility: many trades get clients via Facebook Marketplace groups ("Ofrezco servicios Asunción" type groups)

## 4. WhatsApp Business (not regular WhatsApp)

- Migrate to WhatsApp Business: business profile with address, hours, catalog of services, quick replies, away messages.
- Put the website link in the WhatsApp Business profile (closes the loop for schema `sameAs` too).

## 5. Ongoing content (optional but powerful)

- One short post/page per month answering a real question: "¿Cuánto cuesta un placar a medida en Paraguay 2026?", "¿Cada cuánto hacer limpieza dental?" — price/cost articles rank fast and pre-qualify leads.
- Photograph every notable finished job → add to GBP, Facebook, and the website gallery with a descriptive caption including barrio/city.

## Top-5 summary template (adapt and include in your response to the user)

1. Crear y verificar el Perfil de Negocio de Google con la categoría "[categoría]" y fotos reales.
2. Pedir reseñas por WhatsApp después de cada trabajo (2–5 por mes, constante).
3. Crear página de Facebook e Instagram con los mismos datos de contacto que la web.
4. Pasar a WhatsApp Business con catálogo de servicios.
5. Subir 2 fotos nuevas de trabajos por mes a Google y a la web.
